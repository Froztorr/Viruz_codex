Chapter 5

Combat, Hacking & Care

Fights are fully automatic, one VIRUZ per side at a time — there are no attack buttons, specials fire on their own logic, and when your fighter falls you just pick who steps up next. This chapter covers the math behind that, the two-stage Hack/Raid minigame, and how to raise loyalty outside of battle.

The seven stats

StatMeaning
⚔️ ATKAttack power
🛡️ DEFDamage reduction — ratio-based, see formula below
💥 CRITCritical hit chance %; a crit deals ×2
💨 EVAEvasion chance % — a dodge takes 0 damage
⚡ SPDDrives the speed counter (extra turns)
🔮 INTMax MP — the fuel for special skills
❤️ VITMax HP

Damage formula

dmg = ATK × power × (1 − DEF/(DEF+140))
Ratio-based mitigation — it can never floor to 1 damage the way a flat subtraction can, and it can never go negative either. A normal hit reads as roughly 13% of a health bar; mid-tier specials land 25–45%; ultimates hit 65–72%.

Speed counter (extra turns)

Every turn, a unit accrues (mySPD − foeSPD) / 45 speed points (0 if the gap isn't in its favor — equal speed accrues nothing even when both are high). Once the counter reaches 1, that unit acts twice in the same turn and the counter resets.

Crit gauge

Fills every time a unit takes its own turn: a flat +0.16 plus CRIT stat / 260. At 100% an ally becomes tappable to fire a guaranteed-crit bonus strike on demand; an enemy's gauge just auto-fires the instant it's full.

🖥️ The Hack / Raid minigame

Raiding another player's base is two stages.

Stage 1 — Password terminal

A Fallout-style green screen: tech words are hidden in a field of junk characters. Clicking a word reveals its "likeness" to the real password — how many letters it shares with the answer in the exact same position. You get 4 attempts; running out locks you out of the raid.

Target levelWord lengthWord countAttempts
< 304 letters84
30–595 letters104
60+6 letters124
Tool: a Ping_Finder (1,500 Bitz, bought at the Tech Shop) destroys 2 wrong candidates on the board mid-Probe — the answer to a late-game board down to a handful of equally plausible values.

Stage 2 — Steal menu

Pick one enemy pet, then pick your loot. Every option shows a success %:

  • 100% → automatic win, no fight at all
  • below 100% → the defender's stats are multiplied up to roughly ×1.9 at 5%, and you fight a single-pet raid battle

Success % is driven by the loot's base value (pricier loot = lower %) and the level gap between you and the target (being higher-level than them makes it easier, shifted up to ±30 points), and it never drops to 0%. Losing a committed raid costs a flat 300 Bitz penalty.

🎯 Battle-tested tips

Stack a mono-attribute team

3-of-3 synergy is a flat +30% ATK/DEF/SPD — bigger than almost any coverage advantage a mixed team buys you.

Poison & Frenzy never expire mid-fight

Both are stacking ailments that only clear at fight's end or on a Cleanse — reapplying them every time they're off cooldown snowballs hard in long fights, especially against Yellow tanks that would otherwise out-turtle you.

Green's double-hit compounds with on-hit effects

28% double-hit chance means Toxin Injector, Data Leech and poison-on-hit specials all get an extra roll almost every other turn.

Freeze is a trap against a Machine-type card

The Machine habit passive resists Freeze/Stoned outright — bring Corrupt or Charm effects against it instead.

Farm bosses where you're under-leveled

Region bosses are the only source of Malware Cores. Pick a zone where your team is a bit behind the level range — the fight is still winnable and the boss re-rolls its spawn zone often.

Read the raid odds before committing

A 100% steal option is a completely free win. Anything lower scales the defender up to ~×1.9 — only take that fight with a team that can actually eat the harder hit, since losing costs a flat 300 Bitz.
