Know what the scan means

Attribute

The enemy's combat identity: Red pressures your health, Green moves quickly, Yellow holds ground and White supports. Build your team with that pattern in mind.

Habit

Color and type reveal the passive behavior behind a creature. The right Habit card can turn a familiar enemy pattern into your advantage.

Zone level

The same region becomes more dangerous as you travel deeper. Read the level range before you step through a gate; the phone is warning you, not decorating the map.

Security packages on rival databases

Player bases can be protected by four AntiviruZ security packages. Higher tiers cost more to install, take longer to break and protect a larger payout. If you are raiding, compare the steal odds before you choose a target.

Boss watch

Rare material route

👑 Region bosses

A roaming boss can appear on each map. It is a stronger version of a regional threat and is the route to Malware Cores used in stage-two evolution.

  • Verdant: IslandMonkey, FlyingFish, RainbowFrog
  • Infernal: VampireLord, VampireLady, FireGolem
  • Galaxy: the strongest threat in each sub-map
Field warning

⚠️ The Mimic problem

A Mimic may ambush a train ride through the Infernal Realm. It is easy to miss because it does not move like the other threats. Keep a heal ready.

Final signal

☠️ Corrupted Captain

The derelict ship's final threat is the one Galaxy enemy with a real skill kit: multi-hit fire, a corruption order and hull plating.

Journalist's advice: a boss is a material run, not a pride test. If the zone level is above your team's comfort line, return to a safe spot, tune the loadout and come back with a plan.
