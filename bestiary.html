  • Hull Plating — self-shield, 35% damage reduction

👑 Region bosses

One wandering boss per map, re-rolled to a random battle zone on that map each time it (re)spawns. Its pool is always the 3 strongest monsters already roaming that region (by a power score of ATK + DEF + SPD×0.5 + HP×0.3), scaled up.

IslandMonkey, FlyingFish, RainbowFrog

🔥 Infernal Realm boss pool
