Gear, Skills & Evolution

Everything you spend Bitz, Dust and drops on: equipment slots and grades, the crafting ladder, consumables, the four fixed attribute skill trees, and the True Form / stage-2 mutation evolution path.

Equipment

3 rollable slots per VIRUZ, named after real components of a computer virus, plus a 4th Habit slot for drop-only cards (see Attributes & Species → Habit system). Any pet can equip anything — level requirement is the only gate, attribute is not.

Habit card fusion

Every Habit card drops as Common (green) regardless of the enemy it fell from. The only way up the ladder is fusing two cards of the same tier and same color+type into one card a tier higher:

FusionResult tierPower gain
🟢 Common × 2🔵 Rarerandom +20% to +30%
🔵 Rare × 2🟣 Most Rarerandom +20% to +30% again
The gamble: a fused card inherits its Color + Type from one of the two ingredient cards at random — fusing a card you like with one you don't might not keep the one you wanted.

Skill trees

One fixed tree per attribute, 16–17 nodes drawn as connected circles. Every VIRUZ earns 1 growth point per level to spend on either a stat node (+n to a stat, up to 5 ranks) or a skill node (unlocks an active special) — a node's parent(s) must be maxed first. Rarer pets unlock a bonus tier at the deep nodes: +0/+1/+2/+3/+4 for Normal/Rare/Epic/Legendary/Mythic.

Stage-2 mutation roll

Reaching stage 2 rolls one of four universal mutation branches — each is a cosmetic skin and a second, smaller skill tree (8 nodes, Lv50–75) layered on top of the pet's attribute tree.

🔷 Overclock

Speed, short-circuit and freeze effects. Lv55 Clock Surge, Lv60 System Lockup, Lv68 Short Circuit, Lv75 Clock Singularity.

🟤 Bulwark

Heavy hitting and endurance. Lv55 Bulwark Slam, Lv60 Brace Protocol, Lv68 Juggernaut Charge, Lv75 Unbreakable Core.

🟣 Phantom

Evasion and ambush. Lv55 Phantom Veil, Lv60 Shadowstep, Lv68 Mirage Flicker, Lv75 Voidwalk.

🟢 Corrupted

Enemy debuffs. Lv55 Data Decay, Lv60 Corrupt Strike, Lv68 Plague Protocol, Lv75 Oblivion Protocol.

Code Parts & drop odds

Code Parts drop per kill, chance scaling linearly from 5% at Lv1 up to 50% at Lv50+ (region bosses roll separately — see AntiviruZ Bestiary → Region bosses). Each part is typed to one of the four mutations; slotting parts biases which mutation you roll:

Formula: each Code Part committed to a mutation adds a flat +30 percentage points to that mutation's odds. Whatever's left over splits evenly across any mutation that got zero parts. Three parts of the same type therefore guarantees that mutation (90%, plus an even split of the remaining 10% only if some type got none) — mixing parts keeps the roll closer to even.

Synchronize (pet fusion)

Sacrifice 2 fully-leveled pets of the same rarity to fuse into 1 new pet at the next rarity tier — a shortcut past the RNG of hatching a rare egg, at the cost of two already-invested pets. A small permanent stat bonus (10–15% to one stat) is rolled once and stamped onto the result, on top of everything rarity/level/gear already grant.
