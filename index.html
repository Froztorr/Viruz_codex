What is MalwareZ? naming note

The game is called MALWAREZ. The creatures you collect are VIRUZ, and the enemy security programs you fight are AntiviruZ — these are in-fiction terms used throughout the game and this codex. You play as a hacker: hatch VIRUZ from eggs, raise their loyalty, level them up, evolve them, equip them with virus-themed gear, then send them out to clear world zones, crack password terminals to raid other players' bases, and climb from the Verdant Realm all the way to the Tabletop Realm.

Start here: read Attributes & Species first — every other system (skill trees, habit cards, evolution) hangs off a VIRUZ's attribute and species.

Quick-start tips

🎯 Build a synergy team

Run 3 VIRUZ that share an attribute: 2 matching gives +15% ATK/DEF/SPD, all 3 matching gives +30%. A mixed team gives up nothing you can't get back from raw stats — a mono team almost always wins straight fights.

❤️ Loyalty beats fighting

Winning battles only grants +1 loyalty per win. Feeding, cleaning and playing on the Care screen is much faster — a single Golden Cache snack is worth 14 wins. Loyal Buddy (85+) is a real power spike: ×1.5 stats and a signature ultimate.

🥩 Cook, don't just buy

Every homemade recipe out-loyalties every shop-bought food, but needs Viral Meat, which only drops from battle (35% per kill) — never sold. Hunt before you cook.

🧿 Save Malware Cores

Stage-2 evolution needs 1 Malware Core (region-boss-only drop) + 3 Code Parts. Farm bosses on the map you're currently under-levelled for — they carry the best odds relative to your team's level.

🎴 Fuse Habit Cards

Two identical-tier, identical colour+type cards fuse into one card a tier higher (green→blue→purple), each fusion adding a random +20–30% to its passive. Don't sell duplicates — fuse them.

🔑 Raid smart, not greedy

Every steal option in a raid shows its success %. 100% is a free win with no fight at all. Anything below that scales the defender's stats up to ~1.9× — read the odds before committing, since losing costs a flat 300 Bitz.
