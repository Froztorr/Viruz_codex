Nyx's first dispatch field advice

Your first advantage is not a rare drop — it is knowing what the phone is telling you. Keep a balanced supply of healing items, build around a team attribute when you can, and read the raid odds before you commit. A safe zone is not a detour: it is where a careful hacker resets, repairs and prepares the next signal.
